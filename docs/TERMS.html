Architecture

How the servers are built

TermMeaning
ServerOne of the G_ autoloads: G_ChunkingServer, G_HitServer, G_TargetingServer. Each only reads and writes the shared data; no server ever calls another one. → architecture
Core serverA_CoreServer, the abstract base every server extends. It owns all shared entity data as static vars, plus the entity lifecycle. It holds no queries. → every variable
LifecycleA_CoreServer.register(), pre_unregister(), unregister() and release_removed_ids(). Always called on A_CoreServer itself, never through a server. → lifecycle
Module managerM_ModuleManager: the object that receives every hit landing on its entity, through hit(hitData). The servers never apply damage themselves.
Shared dataThe static vars on A_CoreServer. All servers see the same arrays, because static data exists once per class, not per instance.
Scratch dataTemporary per-query state (search bests, hit buffers, visit stamps). It lives as instance vars on the one server that uses it, never on A_CoreServer. → hit · targeting
NO_ID-1. Stored wherever an entity id, slot, chunk, target or curve id points nowhere.

In the example world only id 2 has a radius curve; the others point nowhere.

ChainA linked list stored in flat arrays: a First… array holds the head per owner, …Next / …Prev arrays hold the links, NO_ID ends it. New members are pushed at the head, so a chain reads newest first. Slot, center and targeter chains all work this way.
# walk any chain x = First[owner] while x != NO_ID: # use x x = Next[x]
Free listA stack of indices that may be handed out again: _freeIds, _freeSlots, _freeCurveIds. Arrays only grow when their free list is empty.

Entities

What lives on the servers

TermMeaning
EntityAnything registered on the servers: units, buildings, obstacles.
Entity idThe index an entity is stored under in every shared array. Handed out by register(), reused after release.

Column i of every array is entity i.

TeamC_CoreServer.TEAM. Entities of one team never hit or target each other.
OpponentAn entity of any other team.
Entity groupsBitmask of what an entity is (e.g. ground unit, building). Targeting searches for these.
RadiusThe size of an entity. Decides which chunks it stands in and how big it is to hits.
Pending removalAfter pre_unregister(): the entity still acts, but can no longer be targeted or hit.
RemovedAfter unregister(): the entity is gone from the chunk index and every chain, but its id stays locked.
ReleasedAfter release_removed_ids(): the id is free and the next register() may reuse it.
InertAn entity registered without hit profile and targeting data: no hit or targeted groups, never flees. It still stands on the index and can be targeted.
Chunk index

Where entities stand

TermMeaning
ChunkOne square cell of the map grid, CHUNK_SIZE wide. Chunk id = row * CHUNK_COLUMNS + column.
Column / rowGrid coordinates of a chunk. A column is also the middle aggregation level, because the two bases face each other along x.
Chunk areaA rectangle of chunks as Vector4i(minColumn, minRow, maxColumn, maxRow). An entity stands in every chunk of the area its bounding square covers.
Bounding squareposition ± radius: the axis aligned square around a circle. Entities, circle hits and sector hits turn it into their chunk area (_compute_circle_chunk_area()).
Empty chunk areaEMPTY_CHUNK_AREA = (0, 0, -1, -1): covers no chunk. Held by fresh and removed entities.
range(0, -1 + 1) # empty: a loop over the area runs zero times
Membership"Entity X stands in chunk Y". Stored in one membership slot.
SlotAlways means membership slot. Each slot sits in two chains at once: the slot chain of its chunk (who stands here?) and the slot chain of its entity (where does it stand?). → slot variables
Center chunkThe one chunk an entity's position falls into. Every entity sits in exactly one center chain, so a chunk walk over centers sees each entity once.
Membership countHow many memberships a team has in a chunk or column. An entity counts once per chunk it stands in, so a column counts a tall entity once per row. The hit server skips chunks and columns where it is 0.
Group maskOR of the entity groups a team has in a chunk, column or on the whole map. Searches use it to skip empty space: map → column → chunk.
Merge / rebuildAdding an entity merges its groups into the masks. Removing one rebuilds them, which stops early once the old value is reached. → cascade
Column span_teamMinColumn to _teamMaxColumn: the leftmost and rightmost column a team occupies.
Hit system

Words of a hit

TermMeaning
HitOne area attack: circle, directional rect or sector (cake slice). → shapes
EmitterThe entity a hit comes from.
Hit groups / hurt groupsA hit connects only if the emitter's hit groups overlap the target's hurt groups. Separate bit space from the entity groups.
Stop groupsHurt groups that end an ordered hit at the first target carrying them (e.g. a shield).
Height bandVertical extent (bottom, top) above the ground, of an entity or of a hit. Both have to overlap. Ground position is the 2D x/y.
Sample heightThe height a hit is measured at on a target: the middle of the hit's band, clamped into the target's band.
hit band (0, 192): middle 96 target (0, 64) → clamp(96, 0, 64) = 64 target (96, 160) → clamp(96, 96, 160) = 96
Radius curveOptional Curve giving the radius over the height. Its domain spans the height band, its max_value is the full radius.
Curve idIndex of one stored radius curve. Entities sharing a curve share its id.
Radius factorA baked curve sample divided by the curve's max_value, clamped to 0–1. Radius at a height = radius × factor.

65 samples per curve; a hit interpolates between two neighbours.

Effective radiusThe radius a target offers at the sample height.
id 2 (radius 40), sample height 144 in band 96 … 160 position in band = 0.75 → factor 0.8125 → effective radius = 40 × 0.8125 = 32.5
Preferred targetChecked first when a hit may land exactly once. Only if it misses are the chunks searched.
GatherCollecting the candidates of a hit from the slot chains of the chunks under its bounds, skipping columns and chunks without opponents. → gather
Candidate · accepted · chosen · landedThe four stages a target passes. Candidate: near the shape, passed team, removal, group and height checks. Accepted: its silhouette reaches into the shape. Chosen: made it past the order, the hit limit and the stop groups. Landed: was not announced or removed by the time its turn came; only landed hits reach a module manager and are returned as impacts.
Sort keyPer accepted target. Lower lands first in an ordered hit.
Ordered hitA hit whose targets land in shape order (RECT_ORDER, SECTOR_ORDER, or center-outwards for circles). Only ordered hits apply stop groups. → all orders
DispatchHanding the landed hits to the targets' module managers. Happens from a snapshot, so a module manager may fire hits or remove entities meanwhile. → dispatch
ImpactWhere a hit lands on a target's silhouette, as (x, y, height).
Visit stampPer-gather counter that makes "already collected?" one integer compare.
Targeting system

Words of a search

TermMeaning
StateC_TargetingServer.STATE: SEARCH, APPROACH, COMBAT or FLEE.
TargetThe entity an entity currently goes after, or NO_ID.
TargeterAn entity that targets a given entity. All targeters of one target form its targeter chain. The targeter count is the crowding count.
SearcherThe entity running a search.
RingAll chunks at exactly one chunk step count around the searcher's center chunk: ring 0 is that chunk, ring r holds up to 8r chunks. Searches scan ring by ring.
Targeted groupsEntity groups an entity may go after. Priority targeted groups are preferred over normal ones: a priority candidate beats every normal candidate, whatever the scores.
Search radius (chunks)How many chunk rings around the searcher's center chunk a search covers. Ring 0 is the center chunk itself.
Chunk distanceSteps between two center chunks; a diagonal step costs DIAGONAL_CHUNK_COST.
Flee radius (chunks)A targeter within this chunk distance makes the entity flee. NEVER_FLEE turns fleeing off.
Combat rangeReal distance to the target's silhouette at which approaching turns into combat.
Base x / march xThe x a team retreats to / marches towards when it has no target.
Forward sign+1 or -1: the direction a team marches along x. Decides what counts as behind: a column on the other side of the entity's own column, against the marching direction.
ThreatenedA targeter's center chunk is within the entity's flee radius. Makes an entity away from its base flee.
Retreat / marchWithout a target an entity either retreats to its base x (while fleeing, while an opponent got behind it, or while nothing targetable is left) or marches towards the march x. → move destination
FocusA focus target scores a bonus for searchers, unless the searcher is ignoring focus.
InvisibleHidden from searchers that cannot target invisible entities. Turning invisible drops those targeters.
ScoreCloseness + focus + behind + mutual − crowding, weighted by the WEIGHT_ constants.
SettledA search stops early once no further ring could beat the best score found.
